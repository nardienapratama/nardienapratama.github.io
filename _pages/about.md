Hello, I'm Nardiena (she/her). I am a first-year PhD student in the [AI Centre for Doctoral Training (CDT) in Biomedical Innovation](https://www.ai4biomed.io/) in the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh. I am fortunate to be advised by Professors [Ajitha Rajan](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/arajan/) and [Paul Brennan](https://edwebprofiles.ed.ac.uk/profile/dr-paul-brennan). 
<!-- supported by the [UKRI EPSRC](https://www.ukri.org/councils/epsrc/).  -->

I am interested in building machine learning solutions for applications in healthcare. More specifically, my research interests lie in medical imaging and explainability. My PhD is focused on developing explainable and transparent AI solutions for glioblastoma treatment. 

Prior to starting my PhD, I worked as a data scientist at Telstra and a research assistant in the [D-Lab](https://d-lab.github.io/), supervised by Professor [Gianluca Demartini](https://www.gianlucademartini.net/), at the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia. For my undergraduate studies, I completed a joint double degree program in computer science at the University of Indonesia (UI) and UQ in 2021. I completed my Honours Year at UQ in 2023 while concurrently working at Telstra 3 days/week, working on brain MRI segmentation methods, supervised by Dr. [Shekhar Chandra](https://about.uq.edu.au/experts/9654).

<!-- Beyond my research, I am committed to supporting prospective PhD applicants navigate their academic journeys (see [Mentorship & Outreach](/mentorship/)).  -->
In my free time, I enjoy playing piano, listening to classical music, watching musicals, and taking hip-hop dance classes.
